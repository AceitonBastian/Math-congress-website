Posters

Call for Poster Presentations

Participants of Geometric Analysis by the Andes were invited to submit a proposal for the conference poster session.

Poster submissions Submissions have closed The proposal deadline has passed.
View submission details

The call was open to all conference participants. Each proposal was required to include:

  • the poster title;
  • an abstract of no more than 250 words;
  • the name and affiliation of the presenting author;
  • the names of any co-authors, if applicable;
  • a contact email address.

The proposal title and abstract, as well as the final poster, must be written in English. Proposals will be evaluated by the Poster Committee, and the number of available spaces is limited.

Accepted presenters must attend the conference in person. Poster dimensions and formatting guidelines will be shared after acceptance.

Local Information

Transportation and Useful Information

From Santiago Airport to your hotel

We recommend using a taxi or shuttle rather than public transportation. Traveling by public transportation from the airport requires several connections and a tarjeta bip!, Santiago's public transportation card, which cannot be purchased at the airport.

View full airport guidance Hide airport guidance

There are several ways to travel from Santiago International Airport to your hotel:

  • Taxi
  • Shared or private shuttle
  • Public transportation

We recommend using a taxi or shuttle rather than public transportation. Traveling by public transportation from the airport requires several connections and a tarjeta bip!, Santiago's public transportation card, which cannot be purchased at the airport.

Taxi and shuttle services

After collecting your luggage and passing through customs, you will find official transportation counters inside the airport, just before leaving the secure area. We recommend booking your taxi or shuttle there.

Official transportation counters are also available outside the terminal. However, we recommend avoiding taxis or drivers waiting outside the terminal who approach passengers directly.

Transportation can also be booked in advance through Transvip in www.transvip.cl

By taxi or Uber

The trip from the hotel to the San Joaquín Campus takes approximately 30 minutes without traffic.

Morning traffic in Santiago can be heavy, so we recommend allowing additional travel time.

By Metro

Our recommended Metro route is:

Los Leones → Ñuble → San Joaquín

View the full Santiago Metro map ↗

  1. Take Line 6 at Los Leones Station, towards Cerrillos.
  2. Get off at Ñuble Station.
  3. Transfer to Line 5, towards Vicente Valdés.
  4. Get off at San Joaquín Station.

San Joaquín Station is located immediately next to the university campus.

It is also possible to reach the campus using Line 1, but this line tends to be much more crowded, especially during the morning rush hour. For this reason, we recommend using Line 6 via Ñuble.
